Our story

A slow morning in the clouds

CafeCloud began as a single roaster and a stubborn belief: coffee should be precise, never precious. We roast in small batches at four in the morning, pull every shot to a 1:2 ratio, and let the baristas talk to you like humans.

The space is built for lingering — window seats for deep work, a long communal table for arguments about filter baskets, and a patio that catches the last hour of afternoon sun.
